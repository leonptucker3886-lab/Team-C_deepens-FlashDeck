FlashDeck

Welcome to FlashDeck

Flashcards that schedule themselves. Study only the cards that are due, and FlashDeck spaces out the ones you know.

  1. Create a deck (or try the sample deck below).
  2. Add cards, one at a time or in bulk (front | back).
  3. Hit Study, tap the card to flip, then rate it Again / Hard / Good / Easy.

Keyboard: Space flip · 1-4 rate. Your data stays in this browser; use Export to back up.
